:::{grid-item-card} Situational aware robotic and cyber-physical multi-agent systems
:link: https://robmosys.pages.gitlab.kuleuven.be/composable-and-explainable-systems-of-systems.pdf

Author: Herman Bruyninckx and associated researchers, KU Leuven

Bruyninckx' continuously growing dissimination about the integration of the essential five aspects of any robotic application (world models, formal task specifications, perception, control and situational aware skills) and about the *5C software architecture* (The Composition of Computation, Communication, Coordination and Configuration). Foundation of the Task-Situation-Resource meta model.
