Literature references

Situational aware robotic and cyber-physical multi-agent systems

Author: Herman Bruyninckx and associated researchers, KU Leuven

Bruyninckx’ continuously growing dissimination about the integration of the essential five aspects of any robotic application (world models, formal task specifications, perception, control and situational aware skills) and about the 5C software architecture (The Composition of Computation, Communication, Coordination and Configuration). Foundation of the Task-Situation-Resource meta model.

https://robmosys.pages.gitlab.kuleuven.be/composable-and-explainable-systems-of-systems.pdf
Robot kinematics and dynamics

Author: Herman Bruyninckx, KU Leuven

A book on the physical and mathematical models that are used to describe, analyse and synthesize computer-controlled robot motion. Base document from KU Leuven applied mechanics courses in the robotics and mechatronics master.

https://u0011821.pages.gitlab.kuleuven.be/pdf/2009-HermanBruyninckx-robotics-textbook.pdf
Modern Robotics: Mechanics, Planning, and Control

Author: Kevin M. Lynch and Frank C. Park

A mathematically rigorous introduction to robotics, covering robot kinematics, dynamics, trajectory generation, motion planning, control, and manipulation. It develops these topics systematically, founded on the concepts of rigid-body kinematics and dynamics, with an emphasis on understanding the underlying principles.

https://hades.mech.northwestern.edu/images/7/7f/MR.pdf

(See the video lessons along with the textbook.)
